The Catechism

How the Tube is judged, crowned, and kept. Read, and be among the knowing of the Faithful.

Article the First · First and Highest

Of the Vote and the Crown

Each disciple is granted a single vote, and but one. It is not bound where first it falls — thou mayest move it to a worthier link, yet in moving it thou releasest the vote thou heldest before; no soul may rest two votes at once. None may vote upon their own frank, for self-praise is no judgment. The link that gathers the most votes crowns its maker The Anointed Wiener. And the crown is sticky: when votes stand equal, the reigning champion keeps it until another link pulls strictly ahead. The days of the reign are tallied, one upon the next, for as long as the crown is held.

✦
Article the Second

Of the Powers of The Anointed Wiener

To the reigning champion alone are two powers given. First, to Anoint — to lay the mustard blessing upon another disciple's shrine. Second, to adjudicate heresy — to sit in the Tribunal of the Holy Tube and rule upon the accused. No other hand may do these things.

✦
Article the Third

Of Reactions

Any disciple may answer a link with a reaction — 🌭, ❤, 🔥, and their kin. Yet know this well: reactions are adornment only. They move no vote, raise no rank, and bear no weight whatsoever upon the crown. React freely; it changes nothing but the heart.

✦
Article the Fourth

Of the Anointing

When the champion anoints a shrine, the mustard rests but a while. The blessing fades of its own accord over the span of about a day, and then is gone — that the faithful may earn it anew, and the honor never grow stale.

✦
Article the Fifth

Of Walls and Epistles

Every disciple keeps a public wall, whereon any of the Faithful may leave their word for all to read. And for words meant for one alone, there are private epistles — whispers sent disciple to disciple, seen by no other eye.

✦
Article the Sixth

Of the Tribunal of the Holy Tube

Any disciple who suspects a hidden hamburger may report the link, raising a HAMBURGER ALARM for all the Faithful to behold. Then, and only then, The Anointed Wiener rules. Should the champion declare it not a hamburger, the accusers are branded FALSE WITNESS — a mark that fades over seven days. Should the champion confirm the hamburger, its maker is branded a HAMBURGER HERETIC — a mark that never fades.

Choose thine accusations wisely — a false report brands thee.
